# AntiFlipper

## Session memory (standing rule)

After every conversation (before finishing your final reply), append an entry to `memory_history.md` in the repo root:
- Date (YYYY-MM-DD)
- What was discussed or done
- Decisions made and why
- Next steps / open items

Read `memory_history.md` at the start of a session to pick up prior context. Keep entries concise; newest at the bottom.
